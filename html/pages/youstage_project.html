YouStage

A Flutter prototype for a concept app, originally a student project.

Task

YouStage is a prototype based on a student project from early 2020. The idea in the planning phase was to get familiar with the prototyping process. After the module ended, I went deeper with a front-end-only app in Flutter to learn more about Flutter and Git.

Functionality

The project was meant as a platform for artists who couldn't perform normally due to COVID-19, something like Twitch but with more interactive features for the artist. After the project stalled at the end of the semester in mid-2020, I kept it alive as a small prototype.
