Business Transaction Intel

Trintel

A university team project: a web app for a business simulation at the RBZ Kiel.

Task

We built a website for the RBZ Kiel. The customer wanted software to support students in their business simulation by monitoring the transactions of their fictional companies. We had two weeks to complete it and present it to the RBZ.

Functionality

The software provides user roles with different interfaces, companies and their transactions. Users perform transactions for their company and get feedback as statistics, sales history or order confirmation. Administrators can adjust the entire simulation. This describes the state as of 1 April 2022; afterwards a smaller team continued the project with a few changes.
