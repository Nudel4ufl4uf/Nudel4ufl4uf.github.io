NudelCloud

My own public cloud based on Nextcloud, with a wide range of functions and apps.

Task

With this small project I deployed my own cloud, based on Nextcloud.

Functionality

The cloud storage is self-hosted at a German server provider, which combines performance and privacy. Because the servers are in the EU, data protection law is far stricter than for providers such as Google or Microsoft that host in the USA. Nextcloud is also open source and offers extras such as an email client, office tools and a calendar. I use it as the hub for everything important in my daily routine.
